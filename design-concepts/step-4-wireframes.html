Mobile rules

  1. The compact canvas defaults to a fit-to-office view with visible zoom controls.
  2. Filters move into a labeled sheet or screen trigger, never hover.
  3. Analyst profile has a visible back action and a readable full-page layout.
  4. Report controls, data date, and content stay inside a single-column reading flow.
